# Virgil study workspace

- Select this semester's courses from the FINKI SEIS23 course list.
- Each course has books, a Materials tab, a Lectures tab, and optional AI practice.
- Books are named PDFs that can be opened from the top of the course page. A future local desktop version should store large books directly in course directories.
- Materials are named uploaded files or external links. Categories: past exam (with exam date), single question, study guide (also called “bazi”), and other. Links may point to Drive, YouTube, or other web pages. Automatic Drive browsing is out of scope for now.
- Lectures have a date, title, optional slides, and notes.
- Each enrolled course has one of ten selectable colors, shared by its events, labs, and lectures. The calendar month view shows recurring classes as compact event cards without times; the week view shows class times, with a blue LAB badge for labs. Add one course timetable with separate Lecture/Lab times for each selected Monday–Saturday; edit individual slots later. Winter classes run October 1–December 31 and summer classes February 18–May 31, within the semester dates. Up to three exam weeks pause recurring classes, and classes stop when the second exam week begins. Extra Lecture, Lab, and Review Session events may still be added manually. Courses can be removed from a semester with their associated content after a warning.
- The interface uses a compact, flat desktop-program look, a neutral dark gray mode, and the supplied Virgil logo. Appearance is changed in Settings.
- Future work: generate an editable daily study plan from an exam date and uploaded material. AI chat, quizzes, and flashcards remain lower priority than the organizer and planner.
- Longer term: consider a Linux desktop app with local course folders and a small metadata database.
